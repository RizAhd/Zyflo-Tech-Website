# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid size businesses in Sri Lanka and abroad who need a
website, a point of sale or business app, AI automation, video, marketing or social media
run for them. They compare a few providers, often on a phone, and most reach out on
WhatsApp. They want to know who they will be dealing with, what it costs in rupees and
whether they will own what is built. (Confirmed by the owner: audience is businesses in
Sri Lanka and abroad.)

## Product Purpose

zyflo.tech is the marketing site of Zyflo Tech, a one person digital studio run by
Riflan Mohamed in Sri Lanka since March 2022. Success is a visitor sending a WhatsApp
message (confirmed by the owner as the primary action); the enquiry form and email are the
second path.

## Positioning

The person you brief is the person who builds it: no sales layer, no project manager.
Fixed quotes in LKR agreed before work starts, and full handover of code, hosting and
domain accounts at the end.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google search, from links the owner sends and from WhatsApp. The site
is static, built with Vite, React and Tailwind, deployed from GitHub to Cloudflare
(zyflo.tech) with a GitHub Pages preview. Six pages: Home, Services, Process, Work,
About, Contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Seven services: website building, software application development, mobile application
development, AI automation, AI video generation, digital marketing, social media
management. All copy lives in `src/data/site.config.ts`. Any value still starting with
`TODO_` is hidden, never shown. Undecided: social profile URLs, a web3forms key, a
hello@zyflo.tech mailbox, a booking link, the city for local search.

## Brand Commitments

White ground with the Zyflo emerald (#008B48, strong #00753E). Inter and Instrument Sans,
self hosted. Voice is plain, direct and first person. Zero em dashes or en dashes anywhere.
Motion is on by default with a footer toggle to switch it off. No stock or placeholder
imagery. No fabricated facts, numbers or testimonials.

## Evidence on Hand

Real clients: SJD POS (a point of sale system) and mradventure.lk (a live website), plus
the studio's own offline first invoice and receipt tool. Founded March 2022. WhatsApp
+94 78 434 2391. No testimonials, reviews, ratings, awards, prices or headcount beyond one
person exist, so none may appear.

## Product Principles

1. Say who you are dealing with: one person, named, accountable.
2. Show real work and real facts, never decoration passed off as proof.
3. Make the WhatsApp message the easiest thing to do on every page.
4. Fast on mobile data first; motion must never hide content or slow the first screen.
5. Every page stands alone for search and for assistants: its own title, answer and links.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets,
labelled form fields, no content that moves without a way to stop it, and a plain readable
state when motion is off.
